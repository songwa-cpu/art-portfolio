The people behind the work.

Songwa

Creative Director

Songwa is a Congolese-born creative based between Kinshasa and Accra, whose work sits at the intersection of African identity, storytelling, and visual culture.

Rooted in the DRC and shaped by years of moving across Africa — from the forests of the Congo Basin to Accra — Songwa brings a distinctive pan-African lens to everything he touches. His creative instinct was forged not in art schools but in communities: listening, observing, and translating lived experience into something worth seeing.

As Creative Manager, he curates spaces where African voices lead — bold, unfiltered, and unapologetically from the inside.

"I don't explain Africa. I express it."
